ABOUT THE ARTIST

I like being creative, especially with art, because it helps me express myself in a way words sometimes can’t. I enjoy working with colours and trying different styles, and I don’t really aim for perfect, just something that feels right. Pink is one of my favourite colours 🩷 and I like things that look nice and aesthetic. I’ve got different kinds of memories that have helped shape who I am, and I’m still learning and figuring things out as I go. I can be chill or energetic depending on the moment, and I like being myself rather than copying others.
